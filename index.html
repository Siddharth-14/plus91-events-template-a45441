About +91 Events

+91 Cultural Events Society specializes in curating exceptional cultural experiences that resonate with authenticity & charm.

Based in Burnaby, BC, we bring Metro Vancouver's Indian community together through joyful, family-friendly celebrations — from high-energy Garba nights to Diwali melas and everything in between. Every event is designed to feel like a homecoming: familiar rhythms, warm faces, and a dance floor open to everyone.
